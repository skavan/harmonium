# The accent palette — activities & presets

Status: **V2 (2026-09-02).** The V2 canvas (`docs/design/Harmonium identity palette V2.html`) added the BRAND TIER and the derived INK token on top of V1; everything below stands except where the V2 section says otherwise.

## V2 — two tiers, two laws (the brand tier + ink)

The identity ring's uniformity law destroys brands (the canvas's finding, independently verified: Fire TV → mud, Netflix → salmon, Samsung/Google/Disney+ → one periwinkle), because brand identity lives in lightness and chroma as much as hue. So V2 ships a second tier with its own law: **the badge keeps the brand, the wash stays the system.** Brand badge = the brand's own colour with L merely clamped to 0.46–0.86 and C to the gamut ceiling (achromatics — Sony black, Apple white, Peacock — ship raw); brand wash = the identity formula at the brand hue. 23 brand slots ship (14 devices/platforms + 9 more apps); `accent: "firetv"` shares the one namespace with the identity slots, no new config key. The amber reservation applies to IDENTITY hues only — a brand badge is exempt by design (Fire TV ships #ff9900 exactly; do not "fix" it).

**The ink token**: every slot's third line, `--id-<slot>-i` — whichever of `--accent-ink` or white scores higher WCAG contrast on the badge; a measurement, never authored (worst case YouTube 4.46:1; identity slots all ride dark ink). Consumed by the badge circle (`--tink`), and tiles.js runs the same measurement in JS for the second-class custom hex. `tools/gen-identity-palette.mjs` now writes ALL THREE blocks in place — the tokens (tokens.css), the class map (grid.css @identity-classes), and ID_SLOTS (tiles.js) — one source of truth, byte-compare-fenced.

**Title-cell recut (supersedes the bottom-aligned baseline)**: a 102px cell cannot hold an 18px corner mark AND a full two-line label, so the mark gets its own right-aligned row and the label sits top-aligned under it, full width, with `word-wrap: break-word` (clamp only ellipsises vertically — a too-long word was being cut silently). The shared baseline is the price; full-width honest lines are the purchase.

**Apps** (canvas §7): sixteen brand washes cluster into one blue and one red, so apps default to icon-basic — the slot tints the glyph, no wash; colour's job in an app grid is recognition, not separation, and the JOINT law keeps it legible: no two apps may share both glyph and hue family (fenced). Every stock app identity carries its brand slot (YouTube TV references `youtube`; tv_gen corrected to tv; a load-heal upgrades untouched stock rows). Studio: the Accent control is a tabbed popover — **Accents | Brands** at the top (his ask: two tabs, not one huge list; the first cut's native optgroups were still one long scroll), a two-column swatch grid where every dot rides an 18px dark chip with a ring so white (Apple TV) and black (Sony) both read, None + ✎ Custom in the footer, and the grid capped at 300px with its own scroll. It opens on the current value's tier. The Apps Master list rows gained the same picker. `docs/design/identity-palette-shipped-defaults.md` describes the V1 base this recut built on.

Status of V1 below: **RULED (2026-09-01, V1).** Companion to the control language, distilled from `docs/design/Harmonium identity palette - presets activities V1.html`. Devices have a value and one correct way to show it; activities and presets are things a person *named*, and a column of identical rows is useless as a memory aid. This doc is the curated colour palette plus the treatments that consume it — and the four rulings that shaped the build.

## The constraint everything obeys

The orange ring is d-pad focus — where OK will land — the most important pixel on the screen. No identity colour may compete with it. That is why the palette is curated, not free: an arbitrary picker will eventually hand you amber, and an amber tile under an amber ring destroys the one signal the operator navigates by.

## The palette (eight slots, one name each)

Coral 20 · Fern 127 · Jade 145 · Indigo 272 · Violet 299 · Orchid 326 · Rose 353 · Slate (chroma 0, the default). A slot is a NAME in config (`accent: "indigo"` — round 4 renamed the word to ACCENT everywhere, matching the v0.48.3 activity accent; `identity`/`identity_style` stay compat-read and the Studio heals them on load), never a colour — the theme derives everything: badge oklch(0.68 0.15 H), wash oklch(0.55 0.14 H) — activity blooms at `--awash-a` 0.3 resting / `--awash-a-on` 0.4 running (round 8, panel-tuned), preset pools at `--wash-a-on` 0.75. The FLAT tint runs its own softer pair — `--wash-a-tint` 0.15 / `--wash-a-tint-on` 0.25 — re-tuned round 6 ("still too strong" at .25/.4): a wash covering the whole tile needs less alpha than a bloom that fades out.

**Reserved arcs — never add a slot inside them:** 40°–110° belongs to the focus ring's amber; 170°–240° is excluded on gamut grounds (sRGB clips chroma 0.14 through the cyans). Every shipped hue survives sRGB at chroma ≥ 0.139 with < 0.3° drift; a new hue must be resolved, converted back and measured before it ships, and the fence enforces exactly that.

**The Chromium-61 resolution (ruling: "why don't we just turn oklch into rgba"):** the panel cannot parse oklch, so the math runs at build time. `tools/gen-identity-palette.mjs` emits the sRGB block between the `@identity-palette` markers in tokens.css (`--id-<slot>-b` badge hex, `--id-<slot>-w` r,g,b triplet for `rgba(var(--id-x-w), a)`); `tests/probe-identity-palette.mjs` re-derives from the formula and byte-compares, so the shipped values can never drift from the math. Restyle = change L/C in the generator, rerun, repaste.

## Custom colours are second-class (ruling)

Existing activity colours are honored: the legacy `color` hex still works — its badge is the hex and its wash derives from the hex — but it is second-class by design: in the Studio it is the ✎ Custom row at the bottom of the Identity dropdown. A held custom hex is NEVER zapped by picking a slot (round-2 ruling) — the slot merely wins while chosen (the engine's precedence when both keys exist), so choosing Custom again brings the old colour straight back. Only the explicit None clears both. No migration forced; no colours stripped.

## The treatments are additive styles, like variants (ruling)

**This pass keeps OUR sizes** (ruling #3): no new chassis, no 40px badge, no 17px name — the styles dress today's tiles. `accent_style` on a tile, inherited from its section like every other section style default (`sectionDressTile`), and passed by the activities/presets generators to their children.

The style names (round 5 — "every style has both text and icon, so these style names are not great"): **Basic · Tint · Bloom**, plus the preset **Title** layout (the name leads the cell, the small mark keeps the corner) composing with the same washes — Title, Title + tint, Title + bloom. Config values basic|tint|bloom|title|title-tint|title-bloom; every icon-*/text-* first-cut spelling stays a synonym in the engine and heals in the Studio. Activities — `icon-basic` (the silent default: today's tile, a slot only tints the glyph — and the badge does NOT change with state, resting is already full colour), `icon-tint` (the flat wash, A1), `icon-bloom` (the badge-anchored radial, A2 — the canvas's pick: colour fades out by 72% of the width, so the text sits on bare tile at full contrast, and colour owns the left edge while the ring owns the corners). Focus on a WASHED tile is the ring alone (round 4). And the REAL edge-rim bug (round 5, pixel-diagnosed): background layers sit on the padding box but paint across the 2px transparent border, and the default background-repeat WRAPPED the gradient there — the border strip showed the bloom's opposite edge (activities: right rim; presets: top + right). Every wash layer is now no-repeat, per-layer in the shorthand, and the fence asserts it. Round 8 finished the job from the other side: no-repeat stopped the wrap but left the 2px border strip showing bare `--tile` — a dark rim on the bloom's strong edge ("extra dark border … only on the left") that read as a wrong border-radius. Every wash layer now sits on the **border box** (`border-box` in the shorthand), so the wash runs to the tile's true edge; the fence asserts backgroundOrigin per layer.

A1 carries a rule A2 does not need: a flat wash sits behind the 13px status line and drops `--dim` to ~3.5:1, below AA — so under `icon-tint` the status line rides `--text` (and still goes accent when running).

Running lifts one knob — `--wa` from `--wash-a` to `--wash-a-on` — and the status line goes accent. That orange text is the one deliberate exception to accent-means-focus: accent as a ring is navigation, accent as text is state, and they never appear in the same role. The badge never changes.

Presets — `icon-basic` (today's icon square; a logo card keeps its logo — a Netflix wordmark is stronger identity than any slot), `text` (the name gets the cell — and a SMALL 18px slot-coloured mark keeps the corner, top-right, where the label never reaches (ruled 2026-09-01: "the loss of the icon is not so nice — but I wouldn't want to make them bigger"): 14/600, two lines, bottom-aligned so one- and two-line names share a baseline; ~102×60 three-up — 102 not 92, because a preset cell is full-bleed where the control-language trio pays card padding; same grid, different container, do not "correct" it), `text-bloom` (the wash anchored bottom-left under the label; a preset has no running state, so it always uses the lifted alpha). The preset bloom is the CANVAS-PROPORTIONED pool (round 6): the reference 90×70 ellipse of the 102×60 cell as fractions — 88% × 117% at 20% 100%, fade at 78% — restored once the edge rims proved to be the background-repeat wrap, not the size (round 4 had shrunk it and the bloom read "horizontally smaller than the reference"). The activity bloom was pixel-compared against the canvas's own oklch render and matches within 2 RGB units at every sample — it IS the reference. `icon-bloom` on a preset takes the same pool — the activity form's badge-anchored ellipse assumes a left badge a preset lacks. Text cells keep their band's height (round 4: "the two text versions shrink the tiles … a mistake") — the 60px cell is the 3-up's own, from its section, not the style's. `text-tint` composes the text layout with the soft flat tint.

**Set icons scale like glyphs (round 7):** the phu/custom icon-set renderers (`.ic.icsvg`, `.ic.icmask`) were fixed 24×24 boxes, so a set icon sat undersized in a 30px preset square and oversized as an 18px Title mark beside its material sisters. They are now `1em` square with a 24px base font-size, so every context that sizes `.ic` by font-size — preset square 30, app 38, artwork 52, Title mark 18, the row icon zone — sizes every icon kind identically. Round 8 fixed the half that box-sizing could not: every pack pads its grid differently (brand marks carry safe margins), so a phu icon at 1em still rendered smaller INK than a material glyph. Minted icons are now ink-fit at render — the path's own bounding box is measured once (getBBox, a tick after mount, cached per ref) and the viewBox re-cut around it at the material live-area ratio (ink ≈ 86% of the box) — so every set icon sits at the same optical size as its material sisters. A WIDE mark (a wordmark logo, aspect well past square) fits by the geometric mean of its ink instead of its long side, at its TRUE aspect: the viewBox keeps the ink's real proportions and the host span is widened to match (in em), so layout knows the mark's actual width — square-fitting a 3:1 firetv left a sliver, and the first cut's horizontal overflow crowded the right-anchored Title mark against the tile edge (round 8 tweak: "the sonos icon is too tight to the right"). On top of the auto sits ONE taste knob, `--ic-scale` (default 1, set icons only — material is the reference the auto matches): css_vars on a tile, its section (bands inherit), or a theme. Minted icons paint `currentColor`: a slot accent tints them via `--tacc` (a per-tile `css_vars` `--tacc` override recolours the glyph without touching the wash, which rides `--idw`).

## The knobs (round 7 — "educate me on where can I play with the style")

Every wash dimension is a CSS custom property with a default, so the geometry is tunable without touching a shorthand. Activity bloom: `--bloom-w` (150px), `--bloom-h` (100px), `--bloom-x` (34px — the ellipse centre, the badge's seat), `--bloom-fade` (72% — where colour reaches zero). Preset pool: `--pbloom-w` (88%), `--pbloom-h` (117%), `--pbloom-fade` (78%), anchored at 20% 100%. Alphas: `--awash-a` 0.3 / `--awash-a-on` 0.4 (activity blooms — split from the preset pair in round 8, when panel-tuning showed the computer screen renders far more subdued than the remote), `--wash-a-on` 0.75 (preset pools), `--wash-a-tint` 0.15 / `--wash-a-tint-on` 0.25 (flat tint). Round 8 also baked the panel-tuned activity geometry as the defaults: `--bloom-w` 180px, `--bloom-x` 64px — measured on the real device, which outranks any canvas. Icons: `--ic-scale` (default 1, set icons only) multiplies the auto ink-fit; `--title-mark` (default 18px) sizes EVERY Title-mode corner mark at once, all icon kinds. Type: `--preset-lbl` (default 11px) sizes the Icon-mode preset label (it was a hard 11px, unreachable by any knob); its weight rides the global `--fw-1`, section-scopeable like everything else. Three places to set them: a section's **CSS variables** box (e.g. `{"--bloom-w": "200px"}` scopes to that section's tiles), a theme's style block (panel-wide), or live in the panel's devtools on `:root` while tuning. They live in tokens.css beside the palette. Round 8 made the section box actually reach a BAND: the section dressed only the generator tile, and its generated children were born after the dressing — so `css_vars` (and h / label_pos / image_opacity) never landed on a generated activity or preset tile. Both walks (render and state re-derivation) now dress each generated child with its dressed parent; per-tile still wins.

## Config & Studio

Activity: `accent: "<slot>"` beside the legacy `color`, and `accent_style` on the activity itself (its generator carries it). Preset: same keys on the preset. Style ladder: the item's own `accent_style` → its section's (Section settings → "Identity style", via sectionDressTile) → the silent icon-basic default; an explicit `icon-basic` is a real opt-out inside a styled section. Studio: a slim Accent dropdown — colour dot + slot names, ✎ Custom at the bottom — with the Style dropdown beside it, in the activity identity strip and in the preset header (round 5: "just like activities, not in a tab"). Round 7 re-cut both headers as TWO rows — Display name + Id up top, then Icon · Accent · Style as the look row ("Having ID span the entire row is the wrong one expand") — and the preset Style labels lead with what the tile leads with: **Icon basic · Icon tint · Icon bloom**, then Title · Title + tint · Title + bloom (config values unchanged). The Accent dropdown's dot is derived from the RESOLVED selection (slot hex, custom hex, or transparent), fixing the dark-dot-beside-Coral bug.
